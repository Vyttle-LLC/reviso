# Trademarks

The Reviso source code is licensed under Apache-2.0. **The name and the logo
are not.**

Apache-2.0 §6 explicitly grants no trademark rights, and we rely on that. Here
is what it means in practice.

## You may, without asking

- Use, modify, fork, and redistribute the code under Apache-2.0
- Run Reviso anywhere, including in your own CI, on your own key or subscription
- Say your project "uses Reviso" or "is built on Reviso"
- Write about Reviso, review it, compare it to other tools, criticise it
- Use the name to refer to this project accurately

## You may not, without written permission

- Name your product, service, or company "Reviso" or anything confusingly similar
- Use the Reviso diff mark or wordmark as your own branding
- Imply your fork or service is official, endorsed by, or affiliated with Vyttle
- Offer a hosted service under the Reviso name

## Why we do it this way

We want the code to be genuinely open — fork it, ship it, sell services around
it. What we're protecting is that when someone says "Reviso," our users know
what they're getting. That's a confusion problem, not a copying problem, and
trademark is the right tool for it.
